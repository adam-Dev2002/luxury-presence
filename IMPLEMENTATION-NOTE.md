# Pahrump Residence

This homepage adapts the actual free **TemplateMo 591 Villa Agency** HTML/CSS base to Marci Metzger's business. It keeps the source's white header and flex navigation, full-width photographic banner, Bootstrap 4/5/3 featured section, and property-card structure. Those components now contain Marci's real introduction, buying and selling guidance, and known professional facts rather than the template's sample properties, prices, locations, or promotional claims.

The main design decision is to turn the broad real estate banner into a cinematic view of Pahrump while placing immediate **Find a home**, **Sell your home**, and **Call Marci** actions above a compact trust panel. Nearly three decades of experience and the explicitly dated 2021 results stay visible near the opening. A navy, warm ivory, and muted gold palette relates to the actual Ridge Realty branding. The three original selling stories, an easy preference form, and a seven-photo gallery lead into services and contact without a splash screen or hidden content.

All 21 images are official downloaded assets from `assets/`, documented in `assets/sources.json`. All seven gallery images retain their original order. Original substantive service and selling copy, address, phone number, office hours, appointment note, and all seven listing fields are preserved. Photos are not represented as available listings.

The footer follows the user's dark multi-column real estate reference. A white version of the original logo, a concise introduction, four official social icons, and the original copyright sit beside Explore, Our Services, and Call or Visit columns. These use real homepage anchors and verified business details; no sample blog, membership, FAQ, download, or invented legal links are included. Brokerage and professional marks remain beneath Marci's introduction, retaining every original image. Inline SVG social links have accessible labels and 48px targets; text links have 44px targets. The four columns become two on tablet and reorganize into readable groups on mobile. Source attribution remains documented outside the visitor flow.

Tools: semantic HTML, adapted TemplateMo CSS, the actual Bootstrap grid rules shipped with the template, custom CSS, and shared vanilla JavaScript in `main.js`. Unneeded template JavaScript, font icons, carousels, preloader, tabs, and sample images are omitted. Native dialog controls handle the photo gallery. The shared script handles the mobile menu, motion preference, home-search inquiry, and truthful copy-to-official-contact handoff.

Phone and official contact links are always available. With JavaScript disabled, preparation forms are hidden and direct contact alternatives remain. Form controls use 16-pixel text and action targets are at least 44 pixels tall. Reduced-motion settings are honored and decorative photo motion can be paused.

Validation: local asset references, original paragraph preservation, gallery order, required facts and form fields, link anchors, shared-script selectors, CSS braces, and HTTP responses were checked from source. Automated browser launch was unavailable in this environment; rendered visual and interaction checks remain necessary. Template sources, reuse details, permissions, and hashes are recorded in `TEMPLATE-SOURCE.md` and `template-provenance.json`.
